<H2>Summary</H2>

<Prose>
  The FAQ collects answers to common questions that do not fit naturally into the linear learning path.
  It is organized by topic and cross-linked to the relevant tutorials, how-to guides, reference pages, and
  concept articles so you can jump directly to deeper coverage.
</Prose>

<H2>What problem it solves</H2>

<Prose>
  Readers often arrive with a specific question: “How does ShardScript compare to language X?”,
  “Why does it not have feature Y?”, or “What is planned next?”. The main documentation is structured to
  teach the language step by step, so edge-case and policy answers need a separate home where they can be
  found quickly without disrupting the learning flow.
</Prose>

<H2>How this FAQ is organized</H2>

<Prose>
  Each FAQ article is a self-contained concept page. The index below groups the questions by theme and
  points to the article that answers them.
</Prose>
